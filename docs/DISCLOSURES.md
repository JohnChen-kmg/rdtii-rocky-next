# DISCLOSURES — the honest ledger

House rules, applied throughout: no estimate is presented as a measurement;
every dollar figure traces to logged token counts; a disclosed gap beats an
undisclosed one. Each item below says where the evidence lives.

**(a) Extraction limitations.** 18 + 3 Australian documents are
under-segmented and 218 documents have schedule-extraction gaps. None of these
gaps is pillar-scoped, and the affected full text remains keyword-reachable in
`source_text/` (Release asset `handoff2_source_text.zip`).
Evidence: `stages/p2-extract/docs/HANDOFF2_NOTES_*.md` (limitations sections).

**(b) Malaysia recall.** Recall vs the 51-row baseline gold is **0.727 raw /
0.889 on parsed documents**: two 2017 Codes of Practice parse-failed upstream
in P2, so no downstream stage could find their rows. Disclosed, not patched.
Evidence: `submission/reports/eval_report_MY.json`; per-document status in
`doc_status.jsonl` (Release asset `handoff2_core.zip`).

**(c) Baseline soft channels.** The scoring chain itself (triage → verdict →
blind verify) is baseline-blind — audited. Three soft channels around it are
disclosed: exemplar-guided retrieval queries; threshold calibration against
the gold recall gate; baseline-aware output curation. The SG evaluation is
partially in-sample. Evidence: `stages/p3-map/workflow/` (mechanism + log);
record-level audit in `submission/audit/`.

**(d) Score divergences from the baseline.** Baseline-consistent except
**5 argued cells**: SG P6-I2 up, MY P6-I2 up, AU P6-I2 up, AU P6-I1 up, and
AU P7-I2 down (the Cyber Security Act 2024 post-dates the Round-1 baseline).
Each is argued from cited provisions, not asserted — the four upward cells
(P6-I2 in all three economies, plus AU P6-I1) each rest on >=2 distinct
verified half-point measures, all visible as rows in the judged CSVs
themselves (as well as in the economy-score reports and the review
workbook), so every escalated score is auditable from the deliverable
alone. Evidence: `submission/records_{SG,MY,AU}.csv`;
`submission/reports/economy_scores_{SG,MY,AU}.json`;
`submission/RDTII_P3_results_{SG,MY,AU}.xlsx`.

**(e) Cost figures are measured and corpus-version-bound.** P2
extraction+tagging: **$177.5224** — exactly its own ledger sum (6.6¢/law,
$0.00043/provision; Batches API −50%; 99,299 tags grafted at $0 on re-runs).
P3 mapping: **$281.59** evidenced cumulative ledger incl. re-runs and A/B;
marginal batch-lane cost MY $33 / AU $64 per economy; crawl ~$0. Reconstructed
ledger entries are labeled as such. The 20-economy × 12-pillar figure
(~$8–10k first pass; refreshes are deltas) is a **PROJECTION**, labeled.
Evidence: `cost_report.json` (P2 stage copy); `submission/reports/cost_ledger.json`.

**(f) No cross-provider output parity claimed.** The claim is identical
citations plus a disclosed tag delta. Measured parity where it exists: the
deterministic extraction core produced 309/309 byte-identical records across
qwen2.5:14b, llama3.1:8b, and Haiku. The keyless fallback completes but is
measured slow: ~7–11 s/provision (llama3.1:8b). Tested-and-rejected
alternatives (qwen triage FN 16%; DeepSeek V4 Flash/Pro triage FN 34.0%/28.8%
vs the <5% gate, mapper grounding 48.4%/71.7% vs the 98% bar) are documented,
with the pre-registered gates. Evidence: `stages/p3-map/docs/ab/`.

**(g) Web-research policy — adopted, deliberately not applied.** A
secondary-sources policy (guide-never-cite) was adopted 2026-07-16 but
deliberately NOT applied in Round 1: every Round-1 citation resolves to
primary legal text only. A methodology point for the next round, not a gap.
Evidence: every Source URL / Verbatim Snippet in `submission/records_*.csv`
is a primary source; URL liveness 94/94 (re-run 2026-07-19).

**(h) Host-template inconsistencies — noted, not silently resolved.** The
template carries a required-status conflict between Law Number and Last
Amended, and its Indicator Reference tab does not match the authoritative
RDTII 2.1 guide. We follow the guide's indicator definitions.
Evidence: `stages/p0-instrument/` (instrument built from the guide's
definitions; 9 indicators).

**(i) Prior-work disclosure.** The Singapore/Australia portal-access layer in
P1 is built on the author's own 2025 web-scraping work — evolved, not vendored
verbatim: publicly-observable transport recipes reimplemented cleanly, with
contract-compliant provenance capture added. No third-party or restricted code.
Evidence: `stages/p1-scrape/README.md`, "Provenance & license".

**(j) Demo slices are curated — and say so.** `--demo`, `--quick`, and
`--mini-run` operate on curated documents chosen to demonstrate the pipeline;
their outputs are stamped "demonstration slice — NOT the judged submission
records". The judged records live only in `submission/` and are never
regenerated by a demo mode. Evidence: demo output headers; `submission/`.

**(k) Malaysia amendment-act discovery gap — found by our own audit on
submission day.** P1's Malaysia discovery enumerates LOM **principal** act
numbers only; the A-numbered amendment series was never enumerated (only the
seeded A1727 PDPA (Amendment) Act 2024 entered the corpus). A submission-day
sweep of the series (A1728–A1782, fetched live 2026-07-20 via the same static
`act-detail.php` route the crawler already uses) found **six amendment acts to
laws that appear in our own records**: SOSMA A1736 (2024), Electricity Supply
A1739 / A1775 (2024 / 2025), Communications and Multimedia A1743 (pub. 10 Feb
2025), Criminal Procedure Code A1751 (2025), AMLA A1761 (2025, pub. 27 Feb
2026). Consequences, stated plainly: (i) provisions introduced by these acts
were not searched, so any NEW rows they carry are absent; (ii) the CMA 1998
rows quote the portal's consolidated reprint whose stated currency is
31 January 2002 — byte-true to the cited document, but predating A1743;
(iii) most MY `Last Amended` cells are blank because that field is grounded
only from the frozen document's own text and Malaysian consolidations print no
currency line — blank means "not stated in the source", not "never amended".
**Why disclosed rather than patched:** the gap was found hours before the hard
deadline. The code fix is small (extend the existing 1..900 enumeration loop
to A-numbers — same route), but running it AND carrying the results through
the full crawl → freeze → extract → map → verify chain could not be completed
and verified in the time remaining, and adding rows outside that chain would
break the provenance guarantee this tool exists to provide. The gap therefore
ships disclosed, with the fix scoped for the next round. Evidence:
`stages/p1-scrape/src/p1_scrape/adapters/my_gazette.py` (principal-only
enumeration); `submission/records_MY.csv` (`Last Amended` column);
`https://lom.agc.gov.my/act-detail.php?language=BI&type=amendment&act=A1743`.
