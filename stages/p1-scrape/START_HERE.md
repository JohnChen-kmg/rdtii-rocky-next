> **Read this first — what changed for the submission, 2026-09-29.** This document is Round 1's,
> written when the stage crawled three economies. Four things about this tree differ from what it
> describes, and the differences matter before you run anything:

> - **Six economies, not three.** Singapore, Malaysia, Australia, **Timor-Leste**, **Lao PDR** and

> - **`handoff1_v2/` is not in this tree.** Round 1's corpus was output and did not travel. Where a

>   one folder per economy. `handoff1/README.md` is the guide to it.
> - **`reference/` is not in this tree.** Those two CSVs were ESCAP's own law-to-URL map, and this
>   repository is public at the release tag. A reviewer with their own copy places it wherever they
>   like; no code reads it.
> - **The documents are not in this tree** — 19 GB of retrieved law, regenerated by crawling. So
>   `scrape.py --validate` reports `local_path does not resolve on disk` for every row until a crawl
>   has populated `raw/`. That is the documents being absent, not a contract breach.


**What this is.** The kickoff kit for **Project 1** of RDTII Rocky — the crawler that retrieves raw laws (HTML / native PDF / scanned PDF) from **official government portals** for SG/AU/MY, Pillars 6 & 7, and emits **Hand-off #1** (`handoff1/` = manifest + raw files). **No OCR, no mapping here** — that's Projects 2 and 3.

## The one thing that matters most
Live crawling is a **binary 0-or-10** rubric item — a tool that only reads pre-downloaded files scores **zero**. So **prove a logged live fetch from all three portals early** (PLAN.md task **T2b**, the 3-portal smoke test) before polishing any single adapter.

## Source rule (confirmed from the host's own data)
Crawl **official portals only.**
- **Singapore** — one portal suffices: `sso.agc.gov.sg` + regulators PDPC/CSA/IMDA.
- **Australia** — one portal suffices: `legislation.gov.au` + OAIC/cyber.gov.au/Home Affairs.
- **Malaysia** — a **federation** of official agency sites + the (scanned) federal gazette + a **narrow whitelisted reputable fallback** for the few acts with no reachable official copy. See `sources_my.yaml`. Third-party sites are a last-resort *fetch* fallback, **never** a discovery target.
