**What the walk proves about the section.** 法律法规 holds the laws MIIT did not write. Of the 13 MIIT documents
this folder had already verified by link, only 2 appear on it. The rule tier — 部门规章 and 规范性文件 — sits in the
sibling categories and is still to be walked. This is the same lesson the CAC crawl taught from the other side:
the index of the right section beats any list, and the wrong section cannot be rescued by walking it harder.
