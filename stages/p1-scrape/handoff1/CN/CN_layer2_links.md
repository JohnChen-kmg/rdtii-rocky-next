# Layer 2: verified links

**Compiled 2026-09-20. Complete — all nine publishers.** Every URL was checked by opening the official page and
confirming the document's own header: the 令号 or 文号, the adoption date and the 施行 date. Where a document
turned out to be numbered or dated differently from our checklist, the correction is in the note column, in bold.

`flk.npc.gov.cn` was not touched. Why each document is needed is in `CN_layer2_checklist.md`; what the whole
corpus is for is in `CN_corpus_plan.md`.

**Read the four sections at the end before collecting anything.** Five documents on the checklist are published
nowhere, two were repealed while the checklist was being written, one threshold changed its legal basis, several
texts are attachments rather than pages, and four ministry sites refuse a plain client.

## Six traps in this list

1. **Two documents on our checklist were repealed while it was being written.** 银行卡清算机构管理办法 (2016) fell
   on 2025-11-01; 银发〔2021〕237号 on 2026-02-06. Both rows above point at the live replacement.
2. **One threshold changed its legal basis.** The ¥50 de minimis moved in December 2024 from 海关总署公告2010年第43号
   to 税委会公告2024年第11号 under the new Tariff Law. The old citation is now wrong.
3. **Some texts are attachments, not pages.** 电信业务分类目录 is a `.doc`; the payment caps in 银发〔2017〕296号 are a
   PDF annex; the 跨境电商零售进口商品清单 is a PDF annex; the standard contract under 令13 is a separate PDF. Downloading
   the page is not downloading the document — which is the same failure mode the database itself has.
4. **Three instruments are deltas, not consolidated texts.** 中国禁止出口限制出口技术目录 (2025年第28号 amends 2023年第57号),
   电信业务分类目录 (2019 公告 amends the 2015 catalogue), 电信设备进网管理办法 (two amending orders). Collect the base and
   the amendment, or record the consolidated version where one is published.
5. **Two "catalogues" have no dated document at all** — 实行进网许可制度的电信设备目录 and 中央企业名录 are live portal
   pages. Record the date you read them, because there is no other version marker.
6. **One rule has expired.** 信管函〔2017〕32号 was a campaign that ran to 2019-03-31. It still evidences the policy on
   unlicensed cross-border VPN, but it is not a measure in force.
