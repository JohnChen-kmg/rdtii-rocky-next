# What the five regulations say, read 2026-09-20

Five administrative regulations, downloaded by hand from 国家法律法规数据库 and read here. The question behind
them: **does the 行政法规 tier carry the operative number, or does it state the rule and delegate the number to a
catalogue the database does not hold?**

**Result: 5 of 5 confirm the pattern — and the one that carries a number delegates its other trigger.**

## Document by document

### 1. 电信条例 Telecommunications Regulations (国务院令第291号, 2000; revised 2014-07-29 and 2016-02-06)

**第八条:** "电信业务分类的具体划分在本条例**所附**的《电信业务分类目录》中列出。国务院信息产业主管部门根据
实际情况，可以对目录所列电信业务分类项目作局部调整，**重新公布**。"

The regulation says the catalogue **is annexed to it** — and the database's copy **contains no annex**: the string
`附件` does not appear anywhere in the file. The text also gives MIIT power to adjust the catalogue and re-publish
it on its own.

**Indicator 5.5 (telecom licensing): confirmed unanswerable from this tier.** The regulation tells you a licence
is required and that services are divided into basic and value-added; *which* service falls where — the whole
substance of a licensing requirement — is in a MIIT 公告 that the database does not carry, even though the
regulation calls it part of itself.

### 2. 外商投资电信企业管理规定 (国务院令第**333**号, 2001; revised 2008, 2016 and 2022-03-29)

**第六条:** basic telecom (except radio paging) foreign equity **不得超过49%**; value-added **不得超过50%** —
each followed by "**国家另有规定的除外**", unless the State provides otherwise.
**第五条:** minimum registered capital ¥100 million for basic, ¥1 million for value-added.

**The numbers are here** — and so is the escape clause that makes them incomplete. The October 2024 MIIT pilot
that lifted the cap entirely for IDC, CDN, ISP, online data processing and information services in four zones
operates through exactly that "unless the State provides otherwise", and it is not in the database.

**Indicators 3.1 / 5.2: covered, and wrong on its own.** A reader taking this text at face value would report
49%/50% with full confidence.

**Correction to our own notes:** the decree number is **333**, not 534 or 752 as earlier notes in this workshop
said. 752 is the 2022 amending decision, not this instrument.

### 3. 网络数据安全管理条例 Network Data Security Management (in force 2025-01-01) — the control

**第二十八条:** "网络数据处理者处理**1000万人以上**个人信息的，还应当遵守本条例第三十条、第三十二条对处理重要
数据的网络数据处理者…作出的规定。"
**第六十二条(八):** 大型网络平台 = 注册用户5000万以上 or 月活跃用户1000万以上.
**第十二条:** records of provision to another processor kept **至少保存3年**.

**The control passes.** A regulation can and does carry a hard number: ten million users, fifty million
registrants, three years. **Indicator 7.4 is answerable from this tier**, and 7.3 gains a retention period too.

**But the same regulation shows the pattern on its other limb.** 第二十九条: the national coordination mechanism
draws up a **重要数据目录**, and "各地区、各部门应当…确定本地区、本部门以及相关行业、领域的重要数据具体目录".
The obligations that attach to "important data" — the ones art. 28 extends to large processors — are triggered by
membership of a catalogue issued region by region and department by department. None of those catalogues is in the
database.

**A note on method:** the first pass searched for 一千万 and reported the threshold absent. The text writes it
**1000万人**. The finding was a search error, not a gap — corrected here, and a reminder that a negative result on
a Chinese text is only as good as the character forms searched.

### 4. 非银行支付机构监督管理条例 Non-Bank Payment Institutions (in force 2024-05-01)

`限额` appears twice, both times as **注册资本最低限额** — the ¥100 million minimum registered capital
(第八条). `额度` does not appear at all. The regulation governs licensing, client funds (备付金, 11 mentions),
governance and exit, and sets **no payment ceilings of any kind**.

**Indicator 12.4.5 (payment ceilings): confirmed elsewhere.** The A/B/C/D daily caps live in PBOC
银发〔2017〕296号 and the 2015 account-tier 公告, neither in the database.

### 5. 两用物项出口管制条例 Dual-Use Items Export Control (in force 2024-12-01)

**第十一条:** "国务院商务主管部门…会同国家有关部门**制定、调整**两用物项出口管制清单，并及时**公布**。"

The regulation creates the list and delegates both its content and its publication to MOFCOM. The list itself is
not in the text.

**Indicator 10.4: confirmed framework only.**

## What the five together establish

| | |
| :---- | :---- |
| The tier **can** carry a number | Yes — regulation 3 proves it, twice |
| The tier **usually** carries the rule and delegates the number | Yes — 1, 4, 5 outright; 2 through an "unless otherwise provided" clause; 3 for its important-data limb |
| An annex the regulation calls part of itself may still be missing | **Yes — regulation 1.** This is the sharpest result: `本条例所附` and no annex in the file |

So the generalisation holds, and it is sharper than "some things are missing": **the database reproduces the
instrument's articles, not the instrument's attachments.** Where an index measures a threshold, a list or a
classification, that is precisely the part that does not arrive.

## What this does not establish

Five documents, all chosen because they were expected to show the pattern. A fair test of the *rate* would need a
random sample. What these five do settle is that the pattern is real, that it survives in regulations as recent as
2024 and 2025, and that "the regulation is in the database" does not mean "the answer is in the database".
