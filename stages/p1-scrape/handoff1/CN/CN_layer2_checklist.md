# Layer 2 for China: the instruments the database points at but does not carry

**Written 2026-09-20.** Layer 1 (the National Database of Laws and Regulations) gives the statutes and
administrative regulations. **This list is what those instruments delegate to**: the catalogues, measures,
notices, lists and standards that carry the threshold, the classification or the scope an index actually measures.

**How to read the "why" column.** It names the specific thing this document supplies that the law above it does
not. If a document does not supply a number, a list, a classification or a procedure that an indicator asks for,
it is not on this list.

**Confidence.** Document numbers and dates are from research of 2026-09-20 and prior knowledge; **verify each
number on the issuer's own site as you collect it**. Items marked ⚠ are ones where the number or current status
is least certain.

## Collecting notes

1. **Record the document number and date from the document itself**, not from this list. Several numbers here are
   marked ⚠ precisely because they are the kind that get mis-cited.
2. **Normative documents (通知, 公告, 函) are the hardest to find**: many live only in a news release or a PDF
   attachment on the issuer's site, and some are withdrawn without notice. Where one cannot be found, record that
   it could not be found — an absent instrument and an unfindable one are different facts.
3. **Some of these overlap deliberately.** 4.9 is covered by both the CAC algorithm rules and the technology
   export catalogue; 7.3 by both the CSL and the MPS measures. Collect both: an index that cites only one will
   report the weaker obligation.
