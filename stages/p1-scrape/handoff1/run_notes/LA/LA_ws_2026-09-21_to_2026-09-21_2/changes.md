The gazette publishes as made and never edits a document, so no verdict here says "amended". What it
does say, and no other portal in this workshop does, is **`status_changed`**: every listing row carries a
status word, so a law moving from ປັດຈຸບັນ (current) to ສະບັບເກົ່າ (old version) is visible without opening
anything. An amendment arrives as its own instrument, or as a whole revised text that pushes the one it
replaces into the superseded listing.
